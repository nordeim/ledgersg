## 3. The closure verdict (the headline)

**The credit note finally says what it corrects.** P34 built the full PINT-SG credit note; P35 wired the business-rule layer; P36 made the arithmetic checked-truth — and P37 found the one truth the DB had guaranteed all along that NO emitted artefact carried: the reference to the original invoice. The service layer has refused a credit note without `related_document_id` since P14 (the IRAS rule quoted in its own docstring), yet the transmitted XML named no invoice and the PDF showed none — a receiver could not match the credit note to the invoice it corrects, and the IRAS "number and date of the original tax invoice" requirement was unmet by both presentation surfaces. Post-P37 the mapper threads the FK, the generator emits `cac:BillingReference/cac:InvoiceDocumentReference` (number + issue date) in the UBL position, the XSDs declare the shape, BR-SG-07 pins the presence floor on every CreditNote root (sound — the DB contract), the PDF renders the reference block under a proper "CREDIT NOTE" heading, and the reason note rides as `cbc:Note` (the BR-111-GST-SG surface) with byte-stability for notes-empty documents. Proven live on the real demo credit note: the reference validates clean, the stripped control fails with BR-SG-07 named, and the invoice bytes are unchanged.
